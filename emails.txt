## 2. Dave Freese, W1HKJ — fldigi author
Subject: gtty: a single-mode RTTY terminal that speaks fldigi's XML-RPC

Dave,

I'm Gordon, WA8Q. I owe fldigi a lot; it's how I first got on the digital modes from Linux, and I wanted you to hear about this from me before you see it elsewhere.

I've written gtty, a GTK4/libadwaita RTTY-only terminal for Linux:
https://github.com/gjyoung1974/gtty

It's not meant to compete with fldigi's breadth. It does one mode with a contest-style layout: several demodulators in parallel with a vote on their output, a Gritty-style character correlator, a skimmer that decodes the whole passband, FSK keying over a USB UART, and Hamlib rig control.

The part that concerns you directly: gtty exposes an fldigi-compatible XML-RPC server on port 7362 so CQRLOG, TLF and other loggers that drive fldigi can use it without changes. I implemented the subset those loggers call. If there are methods or behaviours you consider essential for a server claiming compatibility, or anything in my implementation you'd rather I did differently, I'd be grateful to hear it.

GPL-3.0-or-later. Thanks for the years of work on fldigi.

73,
Gordon WA8Q

## 7. Ham Radio Workbench — George KJ6VU and Jeremy KF7IJZ (contact form at hamradioworkbench.com)
Subject: Workbench topic: building a RTTY modem from the published MMTTY design

George and Jeremy,

I'm Gordon, WA8Q. I've just released gtty, a GTK4 RTTY terminal for Linux, and I think the build story is more interesting than the app: https://github.com/gjyoung1974/gtty

Rather than port an existing decoder, I wrote the demodulator from the published MMTTY design: complex mixers at mark and space, matched filters, per-tone automatic threshold control, soft start/stop-bit integration, AFC from a baseband discriminator. Then I ran several of them in parallel and added a confidence-weighted vote, a Gritty-style joint-timing correlator over all 32 Baudot templates, and a skimmer with a decoder every 50 Hz across the waterfall. The station side is Hamlib plus true FSK through a plain USB UART at 45.45 baud 5N2.

If you'd like a segment on DSP for RTTY, FSK keying from Linux, or what it takes to ship a GTK4 ham app with CI-built packages, I'd be glad to join. GPL-3.0-or-later.

73,
Gordon WA8Q
